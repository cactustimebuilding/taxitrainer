Taxi Trainer Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026. Published by Cactus Time Building LLC ("we").

Summary

Taxi Trainer has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, unlocked airports and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them (purchases can be restored with your Apple ID).

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple ID. Apple may share with us anonymous sales totals.

Voice

Air traffic control instructions are read aloud using the speech engine built into your iPhone or iPad. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
