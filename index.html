TAXI TRAINER

Practice ground taxi, before you need it

A game for student pilots that turns confusing ground instructions into practice you can do on your phone. Hear a realistic taxi clearance, then trace the route with your finger. Miss a hold-short and you will know right away.

  • Real airport diagrams from towered airports across Arizona, the Pacific Northwest and California
  • Spoken ATC instructions with the taxiway names, runway crossings and hold-short lines you will see in real life
  • Starts free with Scottsdale; more airports can be unlocked or earned by playing
  • No account and no tracking

Coming to iPhone and iPad. Taxi Trainer is a training aid only. It is not an FAA product and must never be used for real-world navigation.
